Test plan

1
Establish normal operation
Send 2 requests to the normal URL, without a query parameter.
POST /api/payments/process
Expected: SUCCESS



2
Trigger the circuit breaker
Send 10 failing requests, one after another.
POST /api/payments/process?fail=true
Expected: your service returns the FALLBCK response for each failure. After the tenth recorded failure, check Actuator; the state should be OPEN.



3
Verify OPEN state
Immediately send 3 normal requests during the 60-second OPEN period.
POST /api/payments/process
Expected: FALLBCK, not SUCCESS. The protected payment-processing method should not execute.



4
Verify HALF_OPEN and recovery
After the 60-second wait, send up to 3 successful requests without ?fail=true, checking Actuator between calls.
Expected: HALF_OPEN during recovery testing, followed by CLOSED if all permitted probe calls succeed.
